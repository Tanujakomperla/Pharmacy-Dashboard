What Do These Values Mean?

Interpretation of the calculated statistics.

Mean: The arithmetic average of all valid observations.

Median: The middle value after sorting the observations.

Mode: The value or values that occur most frequently.

Standard Deviation: Measures how spread out the observations are around the mean.

Minimum: The smallest value in the selected variable.

Maximum: The largest value in the selected variable.
