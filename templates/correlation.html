Understanding Correlation

Pearson correlation coefficient measures the strength and direction of a linear relationship between two numerical variables.

A value close to +1 indicates a strong positive linear relationship.

A value close to -1 indicates a strong negative linear relationship.

A value close to 0 indicates little or no linear relationship.

Important: Correlation describes an association and does not establish causation.

Dataset note: Percentage is calculated from academic marks. Therefore, correlations involving Total Marks and individual subject marks can be very high.
